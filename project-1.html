BACKGROUND

More content than ever, but harder to actually enjoy.

With dozens of OTT apps and endless streaming catalogs, TVs have become increasingly complicated to navigate. What used to be a relaxing living room habit has turned into an endless cycle of searching, switching, and decision fatigue.

Research in the UK revealed that viewers spend over 100 hours a year just deciding what to watch—turning leisure into a frustrating chore.

In designing Vision AI, we tackled three core challenges:

High Barrier to Entry

Smart TVs assume technical fluency. Layered menus and complex navigation alienate casual users and older audiences before they even hit play.

Decision Fatigue

Endless recommendations often trigger decision paralysis. People spend more time scrolling through rows of tiles than actually engaging with stories.

Fragmented Flow

Every search or setting adjustment takes over the entire display, abruptly breaking immersion and pulling viewers out of the moment.

The Design Challenge

How can an AI-powered TV eliminate browsing friction and bring effortless immersion back to the living room?

Bringing Vision AI to the global stage.

Live Sports Scenario

Real-Time Scene Recognition

Detects on-screen sports action instantly to serve context-aware stats and insights.

Voice Audio Tuning

Resolves viewer frustration with loud commentary or stadium noise by letting users isolate or balance crowd and voice levels with simple speech commands.

Contextual Q&A

Answers live questions (e.g., “Who has the momentum today?”) with instant, data-backed visual cards.

Contextual Recipe & Cross-Device Handoff

On-Screen Food Recognition

Recognizes dishes featured in shows and generates tailored recipes through casual conversation.

Seamless Screen Handoff

Pushes the generated recipe directly to connected kitchen displays, letting users cook without losing their place on the main TV.

MAIN FEATURE 01

Intent-Driven Conversational Discovery

Navigating traditional smart TVs requires digging through deep menu trees. With Vision AI, users simply speak to engage with the system without pausing playback or interrupting immersion.

Zero-Interruption Voice Controls

Users adjust subtitles, switch picture modes, or launch apps with natural phrases like “make the voices clearer”—instantly executed without taking over the full screen.

Contextual Discovery

Casual intents such as “something fun and light tonight” or “who is that actor?” return concise, glanceable visual cards directly linked to what is currently playing.

MAIN FEATURE 02

Vision AI Home: In-Context Multitasking

Conventional TV interfaces force users to choose between watching content or searching menus. Vision AI Home introduces a lightweight, responsive overlay triggered by a single remote click.

→

Non-Disruptive Visual Overlay

Viewers can discover music tracks, identify fashion items, or look up cast members without exiting the active video stream.

Optimized Cognitive Load (A/B Tested)

Rather than overwhelming the screen with endless tiles, we conducted A/B testing to limit the interface to high-confidence, contextually relevant cards—maximizing discovery while minimizing decision fatigue.

MAIN FEATURE 03

Ambient Generative Canvas

Most TVs spend 80% of their day as a lifeless black rectangle in the center of the living room. Vision AI redefines the idle screen into a dynamic, sensing canvas that adapts to the mood of the home.

Prompt-Free Mood Generation

Users simply pick an intuitive atmosphere keyword, and the system generates responsive visuals and background audio tailored to the living space.

Conversational Atmosphere Tuning

Through casual voice prompts, viewers can refine lighting, tempo, or visual themes, turning the screen from a static appliance into an ambient companion.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES & REFLECTIONS

01. Making AI Reasoning Visible & Trustworthy

Designing conversational AI for the living room taught me that pure speed doesn’t equal user trust. When AI presents answers out of nowhere, users often feel alienated or skeptical.

Externalizing the “Why”

During testing, engagement increased significantly when the system briefly showed why it recommended a title or recipe rather than simply serving the output.

Step-by-Step Transparency

We designed progressive disclosure cues so viewers can follow the AI’s reasoning steps, transforming the interaction from a mysterious black box into an accountable conversation.

02. Radical Accessibility: Lowering the Prompting Barrier

Advanced AI shouldn’t require prompt engineering skills. Many living room users—especially older adults or casual viewers—struggle to formulate precise natural language queries.

From Prompting to Recognition

Where conventional AI demands articulate prompts, Vision AI utilizes glanceable, card-based visual affordances.

Inclusive by Default

By putting clear, tappable options right on the screen, we drastically reduced both motor and cognitive load, establishing a truly universal interaction model for every family member.

03. Preserving Agency: From Control to Intentional Dialogue

The ultimate trap in AI UX is creating passive dependence. If the system automates everything, the user loses their sense of choice.

Fostering Intentional Choice

We shifted the role of TV AI from a command executor to a conversational partner that stimulates curiosity without taking over.

Quiet Coexistence

By blending context-aware suggestions with serene ambient visuals during idle states, the screen respects the physical living space, remaining ready to assist without demanding continuous attention.
