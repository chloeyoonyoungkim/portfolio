Product Features

Ballie is an autonomous mobile AI companion designed for the domestic environment. Moving seamlessly through living spaces, it choreographs smart home connectivity, conversational intelligence, and dynamic spatial projection to deliver proactive, hands-free assistance.

Opportunities

While traditional smart assistants rely on static screens and reactive voice commands, an autonomous mobile robot requires an entirely new interaction paradigm. I focused on designing multimodal interactions and spatial projection systems that seamlessly adapt to the rhythm of domestic life.

01. Ambient Presence & Contextual Greetings

Establishing non-intrusive awareness models that recognize user presence and routines without demanding immediate attention.

02. Proactive Learning & Personalization

Designing proactive feedback loops where the assistant anticipates routines and serves information without manual prompts.

03. Adaptive Spatial Projection System

Transforming domestic surfaces into flexible, form-free display canvases tailored to varying distances, angles, and activities.

04. Hands-Free Physicality: Foot-Press Interface

Extending interaction beyond conventional touchscreens by introducing floor-projected controls for hands-busy domestic scenarios.

Main Feature 1

Ambient Presence & Contextual Greetings

A mobile robot that greets users every time it sees them quickly turns from helpful companion into ambient noise. The goal was to build connection through precise timing and restraint rather than relentless engagement.

  • Proximity & Gaze-Aware TriggersLeveraged mobile proximity signals and low-power computer vision to distinguish between passive room transitions and intentional engagement, initiating greetings only when the user is receptive.
  • Context-Sensitive Cue CalibrationEngineered dynamic response rules where greetings adapt to external context — delivering brief, subtle auditory chimes during busy morning rushes, while offering richer multimodal summaries (weather, schedules) when users pause nearby.
  • Non-Intrusive Idle StatesDefined calm resting postures and peripheral staging positions so Ballie maintains an approachable presence in the room without obstructing domestic traffic or demanding focal attention.
Main Feature 2

Proactive Learning & Personalization

Unlike conventional smart assistants that wait passively for explicit voice commands, Ballie anticipates user needs by observing behavioral rhythms and contextual patterns within the home. The design challenge was defining the threshold between helpful proactivity and intrusive interruption.

  • Routine-Based AnticipationDesigned adaptive logic that learns repeating daily habits — such as recognizing a 7 a.m. wake-up routine to proactively surface morning news briefings or adjust environmental lighting without explicit prompting.
  • State-Responsive Behavior ModelingEstablished interaction states tailored to specific user activities (working, exercising, or unwinding), ensuring the assistant offers timely support that matches the user’s current cognitive load.
  • Zero-Prompt Information DeliveryReduced input friction by initiating relevant content — like transitioning directly into guided workout timers or relaxing ambient media when a specific domestic routine is detected.
Main Feature 3

Adaptive Spatial Projection System

Domestic environments are visually cluttered and lack standardized screens. Rather than treating projection as a static rectangular frame, I designed an adaptive spatial display system that treats everyday architectural surfaces as flexible, non-intrusive interfaces.

  • Dynamic Surface CalibrationLeveraged spatial mapping to dynamically calculate surface angles, projection distances, and ambient lighting, ensuring text and UI elements remain legible whether cast onto low floors or vertical walls.
  • Adaptive Visual Scale & HierarchyCreated a responsive visual design system that scales interface density based on proximity — rendering glanceable, bold iconography from across the room, and shifting to detailed interactive controls as the user approaches.
  • Peripheral, Non-Intrusive PresenceDesigned placement guidelines to anchor visual information in peripheral sightlines rather than dead center, supporting ambient awareness without demanding focal attention or dominating the living space.
Main Feature 4

Hands-Free Physicality: Foot-Press Interface

In domestic environments, users frequently find themselves in hands-busy situations — such as cooking, working out, or carrying items — where reaching for a smartphone or operating physical touchscreens introduces unnecessary cognitive and physical friction. To bridge this gap, I designed the foot-press virtual interface, transforming everyday floors into dynamic touchpoints using Ballie’s triple-laser projector and spatial sensors.

  • Floor-Projected ControlsInstead of requiring users to approach a screen, Ballie casts intuitive control menus and status widgets directly onto the floor within the user’s immediate reaching zone.
  • Foot Gestures & Tap AffirmationEngineered intuitive physical gestures — including single-taps to trigger smart home appliances or adjust thermostats, and gentle slides to scroll through media playlists or workouts.
  • Contextual Safety & FeedbackDefined visual feedback and boundary detection rules so users can confidently trigger commands without accidental misfires while moving naturally around the home.
Personal Learnings & Principles

01. Context-Adaptive Error Communication & Troubleshooting

Operating an autonomous physical robot in unstructured domestic settings inevitably introduces environmental friction — such as navigating loose cables, transitioning between rug pile heights, or encountering blocked pathways.

  • Graceful Degradation Over Immediate InterruptionA core principle was limiting active alerts to the absolute minimum. Rather than emitting loud alarms or demanding urgent troubleshooting for every minor obstacle, Ballie first attempts autonomous resolution (e.g., path re-routing or gentle angle adjustments).
  • Multi-Tiered Feedback ChannelsWhen human intervention is truly necessary, error feedback is graduated based on user proximity: subtle ambient light indicators for peripheral awareness, transitioning to floor-projected status hints or companion mobile push alerts only if the obstacle persists.

02. The Emergence of Companion Attachment Beyond Utility

Moving from initial proof-of-concept prototypes to testing in real living environments revealed a profound shift in how users perceive an embodied AI entity.

  • From Appliance to Autonomous CompanionWhile initial user expectations centered on utility (smart home control, projection, home security), prolonged exposure transformed Ballie from an appliance into a pet-like presence. Subtle micro-movements, polite spatial distance, and responsive greetings created tangible emotional resonance.
  • Expanding Beyond General ConvenienceThis dynamic was particularly pronounced among solo dwellers and families requiring additional daily care. It validated that for mobile domestic robotics, seamless functional utility is merely the baseline; the true design frontier lies in choreographing respectful physical presence and emotional companionship.
